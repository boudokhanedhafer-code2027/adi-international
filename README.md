# ADI INTERNATIONAL 🌍

### International Product Discovery & Resale Platform

ADI INTERNATIONAL is a project to develop a multilingual platform connecting customers with international product suppliers.

## 🌐 Our Vision
Make international product discovery easier, accessible and transparent.

## 🛍️ Main Features

- Multisector product catalogue
- International supplier comparison
- French and English languages
- Customer product requests
- Product price comparison
- Human approval before every purchase
- Gradual, self-funded development

## 💼 Business Model

- Variable commissions depending on product category
- Advertising opportunities
- Promotional images and videos
- International supplier partnerships

## 🔒 Security Principles

- No supplier purchase without confirmed customer payment.
- Human approval before every purchase.
- Supplier order limit planned at 50 USD per order.
- Payment and customer data security must be verified before launch.

## 🚀 Project Status

ADI INTERNATIONAL is currently under development.

Online payments, supplier integrations and automatic product distribution are planned features and are not yet confirmed as operational.
