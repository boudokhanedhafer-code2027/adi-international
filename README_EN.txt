ADI INTERNATIONAL — STATIC DEMO WEBSITE

Contents: index.html, assets/style.css and assets/app.js.

Install:
1. Extract the ZIP archive.
2. In your hosting control panel, open File Manager.
3. Open the domain document root (often public_html).
4. Upload the ZIP contents directly into that folder.
5. Confirm index.html is at the root and visit your domain.

This is a static demo only. The contact form does not send email. No customer accounts,
cart, payments, database, supplier, stock or automation is connected. No product, price
or supplier is verified. Have all commercial, legal, security and payment arrangements
reviewed by a responsible adult and the relevant providers before launch.
